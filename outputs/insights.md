# Key Insights — Online Course Learning Analysis

> This project uses synthetic practice data generated with NumPy. The patterns below are for learning the workflow only; they are not real research findings.

- Dataset size: **420 learners** across **5 courses**.
- Overall course completion rate: **7.14%** of learners finished their course.
- The course with the highest average final score is **Machine Learning** (76.46).
- The course with the highest completion rate is **Web Development** (9.09% completed).
- Correlation between watch hours and final score: **0.73**.
- Correlation between lessons completed and final score: **0.79**.
